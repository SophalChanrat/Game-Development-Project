# Forest Guardian: Spirits of the Wild

A small-scale **3D RPG / Adventure game** developed with **Unity** as a university game development project. The game focuses on exploration, simple combat, and environmental protection, where the player takes the role of a Forest Guardian chosen by ancient spirits to defend sacred trees and rescue wildlife from invading goblins.

This project was designed to be **technically achievable within a limited timeline**, while still demonstrating core gameplay systems such as player control, enemy AI, health systems, win/lose conditions, and UI management.


## 📌 Project Information

* **Game Title:** Forest Guardian: Spirits of the Wild
* **Genre:** RPG, Adventure, Exploration, Fantasy
* **Platform:** PC (Keyboard & Mouse, Controller supported)
* **Engine:** Unity 3D
* **Target Audience:** Ages 10–25 (Fantasy & RPG players)

## 🎮 Game Overview

In *Forest Guardian: Spirits of the Wild*, the player is chosen by ancient forest spirits to protect the sacred **Spirit Trees**, which maintain balance in a magical forest. Goblins have invaded the forest to steal magical crystals hidden within the trees, capturing animals along the way.

The player must:

* Defeat invading enemies
* Rescue trapped wildlife
* Prevent Spirit Trees from being destroyed

Failure to protect the forest results in the loss of balance and the fall of the sacred trees.


## 🕹️ Controls

### Keyboard & Mouse

* **WASD** – Movement
* **Mouse Left Click** – Attack
* **Mouse Right Click** – Spirit Skill
* **Shift** – Dash / Dodge
* **Space** – Jump
* **F** – Interact / Rescue Animals
* **Mouse Scroll Click** – Enemy Lock
* **Q and E** - Change Enemy Locked 
* **1 2 3** - Spirit Magical Skills

### Controller

* Left Stick – Move
* Right Stick – Look / Change Enemy Locked
* Right Stick Press - Locked Enemy
* Buttons – Attack, Skills, Dash, Interact
* D-Pad - Spirit Magical Skills

## 🔄 Gameplay Flow

1. Player explores the forest environment
2. Enemy waves spawn and attack Spirit Trees
3. Player engages enemies using melee and spirit abilities
4. Animals are rescued by interacting with cages or traps
5. Tree health is monitored throughout the waves
6. Game checks win or lose conditions


## 🏆 Win & Lose Conditions

### Win Conditions

* Rescue all trapped animals
* Protect Spirit Trees for 3 enemy waves

### Lose Conditions

* Player health reaches zero
* Spirit Trees are fully destroyed
* Time runs out before rescuing animals


## 🎨 Art Direction

The game uses a **low-poly fantasy art style** to ensure:

* Clear visual readability
* Good performance on low-to-mid range PCs
* Fast development using existing asset packs

UI elements are kept simple and functional, focusing on player health, tree health, skills, and dialogue.


## 🔧 Technical Features

* Player movement and combat system
* Enemy chase, attack, and animation handling
* Tree health and damage system
* Animal rescue mechanics
* Wave-based enemy spawning
* UI Manager (health bars, menus, pause)
* Game Manager (win/lose logic)

## ⚠️ Challenges & Limitations

### Challenges

* Managing multiple gameplay systems in parallel
* Coordinating responsibilities within the team
* Finding compatible free assets within scope

### Limitations

* Limited number of enemy types
* Small number of animals and Spirit Trees
* Simple level and map design
